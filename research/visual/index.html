The pipeline at a glance

The shipped foundation feeds two spikes we own; the feature asks widen what those spikes can carry.

Shipped foundation

calibnet v1.4.0 · mainnet imminent

  • Programmable per-data-set authorizer (150M gas, state-mutating)
  • Removals AND termination authorizer-gated → retention pattern
  • Permissionless attributed deposits (feed anything, on-chain receipts)
  • Signed piece metadata, emitted per piece
  • Batched addPieces (one signer per batch)
  • Cheap data sets (~0.62 USDFC lockup, recoverable)
  • FilBeam CDN reads · cursor piece listing
  • Proven fold/identity stack (tic-tac-toe live)
→

We build (in order)

no external dependencies for 1–3

  • 1 · Corgi v1 — deposits = feeding, runway = health
  • 2 · Authorizer spike — BYO-signer writes + declared-metadata rules
  • 3 · Baton v0 + human-in-the-loop relay demo
  • 4 · Paint war (birthday, Oct 15 — reviewed scope)
  • 5 · Signal-gated: mosaic · jukebox · bounty economy
→

Asks widen the road

to filecoin-services / synapse-sdk

  • Crowd scale (1000s of writers)
  • Wallet-free users (passkeys)
  • Consumer apps that can search & scale reads
  • Agent-side economics (bonds, fees)
  • Compliance-grade retention products
