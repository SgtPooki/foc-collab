- **Lead with the P&L punch, not the flywheel.** Cursor's reframe:
  "Builders can't make money on FOC. Neither can we. Here's the math."
  The custody-symmetry line is the sharpest thing in the deck; lead with it.
- **Slide 2 reads as three unrelated numbers** until slide 3 explains why.
  Reframe targets as consequence; label scope on-slide (pod ~$22/mo settled
  vs the $20M network goal FilOz contributes to) so it is not scope-stacking.

  app becomes a larger unpaid liability.
- **The middle drags (old slides 5-10).** Merge "primitives shipped" +
  "what works today" into one "proved in code" slide. Move the moat earlier
  so the unlock slides read as "given the bundle, here's what to ship."
- **Cut the assumptions slide** from the live flow; footer link for skeptics.
- **The ask must be outcome-first and GTM-actionable**: business outcome as
  the header, feature as subtitle; add a GTM-motion + demo-date column;
  revenue capture cannot be the third ask if profitability is the bearing.
- **Commit to ONE metaphor.** Title said "ladder", body showed a flywheel,
  prose mixed turn/bearing/grease. Pick the jammed-bearing flywheel and make
  it the through-line (persistent mini-wheel on later slides).

  "agents + IPFS are the only bets with evidence, fund those two first."
