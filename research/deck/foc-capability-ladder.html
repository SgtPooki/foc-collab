why the wheel won't turn

Success makes it worse: every winning app is a bigger unpaid bill.

For a piece-heavy app (10 TiB, 100k writes/month) the monthly bill splits like this:

write fees ~$313
storage ~$50

Writes ($0.003/piece + $0.008/call) dominate today; storage is small, egress ($0.007/GB) a sliver. Piece-heavy SMB example at v1.4 rates; large-file workloads shift toward storage. Batching work (filecoin-pin #692, recent synapse-sdk changes) is already shrinking the per-write cost, so the size of the bill is not the durable problem.

The builder gets no cutEvery fee above flows to the network and the SP. The app that created the demand captures nothing, and has no native way to charge its users. Native revenue = $0.
Awkward for SPs tooPer-piece, per-call pricing rewards odd packing and penalizes small writes, so the incentives are off on both sides of the deal, not just the builder's.
A free app is a growing liability. A paid app must build its own custody, fiat onramp, and billing.We decline to build that layer for the same reason a builder would: it is hard and legally heavy. That shared refusal is the jammed bearing.

the honest moat

No single primitive is ours. The bundle is, for now.

What FOC offersWhere it already exists
Publicly verifiable fundingon-chain escrows; Arweave's pay-once endowment (opaque only on AWS / Stripe)
Contract-gated writesany L1/L2 smart contract; Walrus policies on Sui
Storage proofsArweave (SPoRA), Walrus (Red Stuff); a decentralized-storage baseline
Delete / shutdown vetoS3 Object Lock; Arweave (permanent by default)
Coordinator-free shared stateArweave AO, any rollup, or a signed log on S3 anchored to a chain

Nobody else offers all of it in one contract system. Our defensibility equals our integration completeness, and that lead is time-bounded: Walrus or Arweave/AO could bundle-catch-up in 12 to 18 months.

Comparisons cross-checked with three coding models and verified against each named product; not a human third-party review.

what frees the bearing

Two rungs of work turn a cost center into a business.

Rung 1strangers can join

Open, rule-governed participation from a link. Missing pieces:

SDK path to access rulesindexed reads (search, feeds)reusable access-control (no Solidity)
Rung 2the app earns

Native economies, consumer scale. Missing pieces:

payment hooks + escrowbatched multi-signer writespasskey sign-inagent-key payments

Batching cuts contract-enforced writes from ~$0.011 to ~$0.003 each for actions that can share a transaction (per-player-signature games batch less). Passkeys and payment hooks are anticipated in the access-rule design; gas cost is unmeasured.
