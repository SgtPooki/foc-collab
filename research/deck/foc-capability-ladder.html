the modelThe flywheel exists. The first bearing is stuck.

profitable builders → apps → users + demand → onchain ARR → SP supply + tooling → cheaper, better → more builders

Every feature in this deck is graded by one question: which bearing does it grease? Most of the high-value ones act on the first: making it possible for a builder to profit.

This matches the assignment GTM already set: map storage needs to FOC's unique capabilities, find where S3 is a bad fit, and pick small niches with breakout growth potential.

Assignment framing: Juan's FDS advice, H2 GTM Strategy deck (July 2026).

Any two browsers on Earth; moves land in 30-60 seconds; no backend anywhere.

A community pet that eats money CONCEPT

Tamagotchi whose health bar is the real funding balance of its own storage. Every feeding is an attributed payment; run out and the protocol gives about 30 days before the data is at risk.

Hand-off chains with signed receipts PROTOTYPED

Work passes between people or AI agents; each step signed, ordered, verifiable by outsiders. The shape our agent provenance bet needs.

Write-once records, like S3 Object Lock PATTERN

Deletion vetoed until year N; $1,000 funds a terabyte for roughly 16 years at the current price list, with a publicly checkable balance.

16-year figure: $2.5/TiB/mo per copy, 2 copies, current list; storage prices move. Corgi window: 30-day payment lockup tail, verified in FilecoinPayV1.

the honest moatNo primitive is exclusive. The bundle is the moat, and it is unfinished.

Three independent external reviews converged: every primitive (verifiable funding, gated writes, proofs, retention, coordinator-free state) exists somewhere else in pieces. What nobody else offers is all of it in one contract system: writes, proofs, balances, provider compensation, and app-level authorization together.

our defensibility = our integration completeness

The reviews' one warning: "without cheap writes, good retrieval, and SDK support, it is mostly positioning." The threats are Walrus DX parity, Arweave/AO app frameworks, and our own write economics.

Sell it as: auditable retention with programmable write rules, no coordinator server, at cold-storage economics. Not as "only possible on FOC".

platform features after thatEconomies inside the apps, at consumer scale

Payment hooks + a reference escrow

Entry fees, upgrades, bounties; apps that fund their own storage. The shipped payment rails support the composition; it needs a hardened reference contract.

Passkey sign-in

No wallet, no crypto knowledge. The access-rule design already anticipates WebAuthn/P-256 payloads; the gas cost needs measuring.

Batched multi-signer writes

Contract-enforced crowd actions cost about $0.011 each today because every action is its own transaction; batching cuts that to roughly $0.003 and lifts the throughput ceiling.

Payments for delegated agent keys

Today only the owner wallet can pay. Fixing this is what lets AI agents pay and get paid without a human in the loop.

Each of these also upgrades every tier 1 and 2 example: the pet gains paid adoptions, the canvas gains event-scale crowds, the hand-off chain gains per-step escrow.

the askThree investments, in order, and a working demo behind each claim

Opens every tier 2 destination; ends bespoke-only building.

Search, feeds, embeddable views; the events are already indexed, the SDK just cannot see them.

Gives builders revenue and crowd-scale costs; the two levers that unstick the first bearing.
