How this works

The corgi is a payer account on the Filecoin Pay contract. Its storage providers are paid from that account by streaming rails, so the account's runway is the corgi's life. It is declared dead while funds remain (seven days of runway), so this memorial page keeps existing through the protocol's 30-day lockup tail. A deposit that lifts runway back over the line revives it as a new generation.

Mood is separate on purpose. It counts distinct addresses that fed the corgi in the last week, so one large deposit buys life but not happiness.

Every number on this page is a live chain read: the account struct and current block for life, and DepositRecorded and WithdrawRecorded events for the feed log, park, and generations. Nothing is simulated. If a read fails, the page says so.
