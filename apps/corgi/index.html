How this works

The corgi is a payer account on the Filecoin Pay contract. Its storage provider is paid from that account by a streaming rail, so the account's runway is the number of epochs until it can no longer keep its 30-day reserve topped up. That runway is the corgi's life. When it drops under the death line the corgi is declared dead while funds remain, so this memorial page keeps existing through the protocol's lockup tail. A deposit that lifts runway back over the line revives it as a new generation.

Mood is separate on purpose. It counts distinct addresses that fed the corgi in the last week, so one large deposit buys life but not happiness.

Every number on this page is a live chain read: the account struct and current block for life, and DepositRecorded events addressed to the payer for the feed log, park, and generations. Nothing is simulated. If a read fails, the page says so.
